GoreeCloud Archive

Preserving the evolution of GoreeCloud.

A curated public record of major milestones, architectural eras, product transitions, superseded decisions, and the long-term development of the GoreeCloud personal and family cloud platform.

Foundation era

Ownership, privacy, and recoverability become the core

GoreeCloud begins as a personal-cloud effort centered on direct control of infrastructure, data, software, identity, and long-term preservation.

Platform architecture

Services become a coherent ecosystem

Networking, storage, backups, monitoring, identity, productivity, and public-facing services are organized into defined system roles and architecture boundaries.

Application transition era

Upstream applications become stepping stones

Early GoreeCloud development uses maintained forks and upstream applications to accelerate capability, preserve continuity, and learn product requirements. Over time, first-party domain models, interfaces, data contracts, release engineering, and application logic increasingly move into GoreeCloud-owned source while attribution and migration paths remain preserved.

Suite expansion · 2026

Focused first-party applications multiply

Projects for Mail, Music, Photos, Video, Drive, Sync, Documents, Location, Launcher, Calendar, Tasks, Contacts, Notes, Monitor, Network, DNS, Gateway, Browser, Backup, Identity, Changelogs, Messenger, AI, Code, and other roles establish a broader GoreeCloud Suite with deliberately separated responsibilities.

Files and device era · 2026

Storage, transfer, location, documents, and device software gain first-party foundations

Drive advances beyond planning into an authorization-first native file platform, Sync establishes a native secure-transfer foundation, Documents begins a dedicated intelligent document-management layer, Location develops a private multi-user tracking model, Launcher grows into a first-party Android HOME experience, and upstream-derived media and device projects become explicit native-rebuild targets.

Design-system era

Glaze UI becomes the shared design language

GoreeCloud standardizes visual hierarchy, interaction states, accessibility, adaptive layouts, and recognizable product-family presentation across web, mobile, tablet, desktop, and TV experiences. Glaze UI 1.4 served as a Stable production target before being superseded by Stable 1.5.0 on August 27, 2026.

Security, privacy, resilience, design, and coordination identities

Five platform systems gain distinct responsibilities

Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, Glaze UI, and GoreeCloud Mesh mature as substantive platform systems rather than decorative branding. Their public operational surfaces become the Security Center, Privacy Center, Continuity Center, Design Center, and Mesh Center respectively. This five-system model is preserved as historical context and is later expanded to include GoreeCloud Identity as a sixth substantive platform system.

Platform governance era · August 23, 2026

Current contracts and evidence become part of public truth

Formal platform policies establish that Glaze UI conformance follows the current Stable contract and that Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, and Everkeep claims must remain tied to implemented behavior, current authoritative evidence, and the correct acceptance scope. Unknown, degraded, migration-required, and pending states stay visible instead of being replaced by stronger labels.

Development evidence era · August 24, 2026

Fold identity and the first persistent Drive milestone land

At this point in the design-system history, the approved Fold mark becomes Glaze UI's canonical visual identity. GoreeCloud Drive reaches PostgreSQL-backed persistent file/folder metadata CRUD with authorization enforcement, while Search, Notify, and Terminal continue separately gated validation work. Fold is later superseded by Facet as the current Glaze UI identity; this entry is retained to preserve the historical sequence.

Native application mandate · August 24, 2026

Original GoreeCloud software becomes the required application end state

GoreeCloud formalizes native-from-the-ground-up development as the mandatory product model for every GoreeCloud application. Maintained forks and adopted complete application codebases become controlled transitional migration, compatibility, testing, historical, or reference sources only. Narrow mature technical foundations may remain where replacing them would materially increase security, standards, protocol, codec, rendering, runtime, or interoperability risk.

Bookmarks native rebuild · August 24, 2026

Bookmarks establishes an original GoreeCloud foundation

An original Go foundation for GoreeCloud Bookmarks is integrated with owner-scoped records, bounded input rules, cross-owner isolation tests, native service boundaries, and PostgreSQL persistence/runtime contracts progressing through validated source work. Transitional application and Browser capture paths remain continuity surfaces, and no native production cutover is implied by the source milestone.

Semantic-color contract · August 24, 2026

Glaze UI makes color a functional language

Glaze UI defines shared semantic roles for interface, status, connectivity, protection, warning, danger, and synchronization meaning while requiring important state to remain understandable without color alone. Security, privacy, resilience, and application authorities retain ownership of their underlying claims.

AI architecture era · August 25, 2026

GoreeCloud AI becomes the native AI product direction

GoreeCloud AI is defined as the first-party conversation, Workspace, knowledge, RAG, research, files, tools, and orchestration application. Local model runtimes remain bounded implementation choices and GoreeCloud Search becomes the first-party Internet-research provider. Transitional third-party AI front ends become replacement targets rather than intended long-term AI dependencies.

Coordination plane · August 25, 2026

GoreeCloud Mesh connects platform relationships without taking authority

Mesh advances as the coordination and governance plane for service and capability registration, explicit relationships, dependency context, policy decisions, lifecycle events, and evidence-aware interoperability. Specialized systems remain authoritative for their own state, and Mesh cannot manufacture stronger conformance, privacy, security, recovery, or identity claims.

Evidence-validity era · August 25, 2026

Stale evidence stops supporting current claims

Glaze UI, Privacy Shield, and Wardveil Security formalize producer-authoritative validity boundaries and CI enforcement for protected or conformant evidence. A previously passing result cannot silently remain current after its validity window expires or the producer can no longer support the claim.

Source-control independence · August 25, 2026

Historical direction: self-hosted Gitea as the planned permanent authority

GoreeCloud selected self-hosted Gitea as the planned permanent authoritative source-control and development platform, with GitHub retained as a migration and interoperability surface. This decision is preserved as historical context. It was superseded on August 27, 2026 by the GoreeCloud Code product direction, which keeps the forge implementation replaceable and uses Forgejo as the initial infrastructure foundation.

Implementation pulse · August 25, 2026

AI source, Mesh durability, and native Search contracts become concrete

The first goreecloud-ai repository establishes native local-AI source. Mesh adds fail-closed scoped private API authorization, producer-bound evidence validity, and durable recovery-evidence persistence. GoreeCloud Search adds fail-closed native category request and readiness contracts with accessible category controls. These are source and contract milestones and do not automatically imply Stable or production acceptance.

Platform refresh · August 27, 2026

Fifty-three repositories redefine the public portfolio

An authenticated inventory reconciles the GoreeCloud source portfolio at 53 repositories: 39 public and 14 private. The public website data is refreshed to include GoreeCloud AI, Code, Documents, Messenger, Gateway, Quill, and Mesh work while retaining conservative lifecycle and production-readiness language.

Design-system promotion · August 27, 2026

Glaze UI 1.5.0 becomes the current Stable baseline

Glaze UI 1.5.0 becomes the current Stable production target for GoreeCloud-controlled user-facing applications. Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, and TV are first-class Stable 1.5 form-factor environments. Glaze UI 1.4 becomes a historical baseline rather than a valid current production target.

Developer-platform evolution · August 27, 2026

GoreeCloud Code becomes the first-party source-control product

GoreeCloud Code defines the GoreeCloud-owned developer and source-control product boundary for repositories, collaboration, CI/CD, packages, security, and AI-assisted development. Forgejo becomes the initial replaceable infrastructure foundation. The earlier permanent-Gitea direction is superseded, while GitHub can continue serving migration, public collaboration, mirrors, and interoperability where appropriate.

Native portfolio expansion · August 27, 2026

Documents, Messenger, Gateway, and Quill receive clearer public roles

Documents advances intelligent document management; Messenger develops private messaging, groups, and calling with explicit transport and encryption boundaries; Gateway establishes first-party HTTPS ingress, routing, certificates, and controlled service publication; and Quill develops a shared typing, editing, privacy, and intelligent-input capability family.

Public Center model · August 27, 2026

Platform systems gain consistent public operational surfaces

Glaze UI is presented through the Design Center, Privacy Shield through the Privacy Center, Wardveil Security through the Security Center, Everkeep through the Continuity Center, and GoreeCloud Mesh through the Mesh Center. The Center names improve navigation and operational clarity without reducing the underlying systems to branding or implying implementation that evidence does not support.

Design-system promotion · August 29, 2026

Glaze UI 2.0.0 becomes the Stable baseline

Glaze UI 2.0.0 supersedes the 1.x production line as the Stable GoreeCloud design-system target on August 29. The 2.0 model formalizes the Canvas-to-Live-Glaze material hierarchy, connected transformation, Navigation Capsule behavior, adaptive appearance and clarity, 48px interaction floors, accessibility and transparency fallbacks, and cross-form-factor design governance. At that point Glaze UI 2.1 remained Candidate; this entry preserves the state as it existed on August 29.

Portfolio reconciliation · August 29, 2026

Fifty-six repositories become the reviewed source inventory

The authenticated owner inventory is reconciled at 56 repositories: 40 public and 16 private. GoreeCloud File Manager, GoreeCloud Maps, and GoreeCloud App Store are reflected in the current public portfolio, while the retired goreecloud-logo repository is replaced by the unified GoreeCloud branding-assets authority.

Platform model · August 29, 2026

GoreeCloud Identity expands the substantive platform model to six systems

GoreeCloud Identity joins Glaze UI, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, and GoreeCloud Mesh as a substantive platform system. Identity owns identity, authentication, authorization, accounts, devices, credentials, sessions, and delegated authority. Mesh may coordinate relationships and evidence but does not replace Identity authority.

Design identity · August 29, 2026

Facet becomes the current official Glaze UI identity

The Design Center synchronizes Facet as the current canonical Glaze UI mark. Earlier Fold milestones remain part of the historical record, but current websites and new derivatives use the approved Facet identity and must not present Fold as the current official mark.

Design-system promotion · August 30, 2026

Glaze UI 2.1.0 becomes the current Stable production target

Glaze UI 2.1.0 supersedes 2.0 as the current Stable GoreeCloud design-system contract. The promotion sharpens the material rule to “Content is solid. Interaction is glazed,” adds explicit Clear/Balanced/Solid clarity and Comfortable/Standard/Compact/Far View density, a 56px Touch Assistance floor, large-text fallbacks, deterministic material budgets, and stronger reduced-transparency, reduced-motion, increased-contrast, forced-colors, and constrained-rendering behavior. Existing 2.0 consumers become migration-required and must earn repository-local 2.1 acceptance rather than inheriting conformance by declaration.

Public website modernization · August 30, 2026

The ten-site ecosystem begins its Glaze UI 2.1 migration

The main website, Suite, Projects, Design Center, Privacy Center, Security Center, Continuity Center, Roadmap, Blog, and Archive are audited against the new Stable contract, current approved visual identities, evidence-scoped product truth, responsive behavior, accessibility, and same-origin deployment requirements. Historical 1.x and 2.0 artifacts remain only where they are explicitly part of preserved history or compatibility evidence, not as active public-site dependencies.

Portfolio and web reconciliation · August 31, 2026

Fifty-seven repositories and the Glaze UI 2.1 web baseline become the current reviewed state

The authenticated GoreeCloud inventory is reconciled at 57 repositories: 40 public and 17 private, including the private Development-only GoreeCloud Index source. The official web model records ten production-accepted public destinations on Glaze UI 2.1.0 Stable, while Identity Center remains an eleventh official first-party surface whose public-domain publication and exact production acceptance are separately gated. This milestone updates current truth without rewriting the earlier 53- and 56-repository historical records.

Capability-family era · 2026

First-party feature systems gain memorable identities

Quill, Waypoint, Beacon, Conduit, Courier, Resonance, and Keepsake organize major application-specific capabilities for Keyboard, Tasks, DNS, Network, Mail, Music, and Photos while keeping the product names intact.

Public web expansion · 2026

GoreeCloud opens ten focused public destinations

The main website, Suite, Projects, Design Center, Privacy Center, Security Center, Continuity Center, Roadmap, Blog, and Archive form a coordinated public web ecosystem without exposing private operational records. Mesh Center and Identity Center remain substantive platform-system concepts even where no separate official production domain has yet been established.

Archive policy

Public history is curated, not mirrored.

The Archive preserves selected public milestones and context, including superseded development models when they are necessary to understand the project's evolution. It does not mirror internal change logs, credentials, private topology, operational details, personal information, or historical material that was never approved for public release.
