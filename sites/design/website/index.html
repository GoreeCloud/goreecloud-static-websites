V1.4 governing direction

Optical adaptation responds. Meaning stays stable.

V1.4 adds bounded Optical Intelligence on top of the V1.3 Stable foundation without making appearance or context a source of operational authority.

Content-Aware Frost

Bounded frost strength may respond to already-derived background complexity and luminance while preserving readability and deterministic behavior.

Semantic Blur Protection

Important semantic content receives stronger optical protection so decorative softness never obscures meaning, state, or task completion.

Environmental Fit

Environment tinting, chromatic depth, and optional decorative color memory remain bounded, consumer-supplied, and subordinate to accessibility and privacy authority.

Material and component model

Solid content. Glazed interaction.

Durable reading and consequential decision surfaces remain solid or optically quiet. Transient navigation, command, search, control, and feedback chrome may use neutral Glaze where contrast and context remain clear.

Responsive and form-factor behavior

Transform the composition, not just its width.

V1.4 inherits the V1.3 responsive and ergonomic foundation for mobile, tablet, desktop, foldable, multi-window, and other supported environments. Navigation, panes, information density, controls, reachability, and optical treatment adapt while preserving task continuity and semantic hierarchy.

Compact

Reachable actions, safe-area awareness, single-pane prioritization, touch-first target sizing, and bounded optics.

Medium

Adaptive rails, split composition, larger working regions, and posture-aware transition paths.

Expanded

Persistent navigation, multi-pane information architecture, keyboard/pointer efficiency, and resizable workspace behavior.

Foldable

Hinge and occlusion awareness, live posture continuity, and composition that does not treat an unfolded display as a stretched phone.

Assisted touch

General 48px interaction floor expands to a 56px floor where Touch Assistance or coarse-input needs apply.

Degraded material

Optical effects can fall away while hierarchy, state, focus, controls, and task completion remain intact.

Accessibility precedence

Accessibility outranks expression.

Forced Colors and Reduced Transparency force V1.4 optical behavior toward a solid-accessible presentation; Increased Contrast suppresses decorative tint and warmth while strengthening semantic protection. Reduced Motion, RTL, large-text reflow, keyboard, pointer, touch, switch, voice, and assistive-technology semantics remain independent consumer-acceptance dimensions.

Exact-revision conformance

Importing Stable is not the same as passing acceptance.

Every GoreeCloud consumer must bind its acceptance record to an exact 40-character consumer revision, exact Glaze revision, and design-system version 1.4.0. Branch names, screenshots without source anchors, generic “latest” references, or shared Glaze CI are not sufficient downstream acceptance evidence.
