- Canonical public website repository: `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-static-websites`

- Canonical branding authority: `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-branding-assets/systems/everkeep`
- Canonical Glaze repository: `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-glaze-ui`

## Transferred static-site authority

The central package owns the Continuity Center public HTML, security headers, robots/sitemap, local Everkeep identity asset, site-specific CSS, V1.3 consumer adaptation, exact Glaze consumer lock, deterministic public build, and public responsive/accessibility source validation.

Historical website source and deployment history remain available in `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-everkeep`, but new authoritative static-site edits belong in the central repository.

## Explicit exclusions

Generated `dist/`, Everkeep runtime/services, recovery and failover executors, persistence, internal contracts/evidence, approvals, recovery-effect authority, and unrelated repository governance are not public-static-site authority.

The site may summarize verified Everkeep implementation evidence but must not upgrade source capability, simulation, backup existence, or a narrowly accepted target into broader Recovery Ready or production-effect claims.

Cloudflare source cutover, deployment, custom-domain rendering, accessibility acceptance, recovery-effect authorization, application-specific continuity acceptance, and production verification remain independent gates.
