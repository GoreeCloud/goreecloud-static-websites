# GoreeCloud Website Branding

Branding authority: `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-branding-assets`.

The website must resolve GoreeCloud-controlled platform, product, and platform-system branding from the unified repository and its `catalog.json`. Website-local public assets are synchronized publication derivatives only and must retain source provenance; they must not become independent branding authorities.

Third-party service artwork remains governed by its own source authority and licensing/trademark constraints rather than the GoreeCloud branding repository.

New or revised GoreeCloud logos, icons, artwork, wordmarks, social graphics, system identities, or product identities must be created and approved in the unified branding repository before synchronized website derivatives are updated. Branding does not establish implementation, release, privacy, security, continuity, or production-readiness claims.
