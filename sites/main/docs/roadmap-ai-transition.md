# GoreeCloud AI Roadmap Transition

Date: 2026-08-27

The public **Expanding the platform** roadmap now treats GoreeCloud AI as the first-party AI application and interface layer.

Open WebUI and AnythingLLM are retired from the GoreeCloud target architecture and are not presented as future GoreeCloud AI front ends. Ollama remains the local model runtime foundation. GoreeCloud AI owns the first-party conversation, Workspace, knowledge, RAG, file, tool, and orchestration experience, with controlled current-information research supplied through GoreeCloud Search.

The public roadmap must not reintroduce Open WebUI or AnythingLLM as target applications unless a later explicit architecture decision reverses this direction.
