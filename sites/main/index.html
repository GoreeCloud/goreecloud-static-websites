How GoreeCloud works

Focused products. Explicit authority.

GoreeCloud separates applications, identity, coordination, privacy, security, administration, storage, and recovery so no single component silently becomes the whole platform.

01

Own the data

Important information stays portable and under the rightful owner’s control.

02

Use focused applications

First-party GoreeCloud products have clear roles and interoperable boundaries.

03

Apply platform systems

Design, privacy, security, continuity, coordination, identity, and administration remain distinct.

04

Verify state

Production, protection, recovery, and conformance claims require current evidence.

05

Preserve recovery

Backup, restoration, portability, and long-term preservation are designed into the platform.

What is GoreeCloud?

More than a homelab.

GoreeCloud is a privacy-first, self-hosted personal and family cloud platform built to preserve a family’s digital life over the long term.

Success is measured by whether information remains accessible, understandable, recoverable, portable, and under the control of its rightful owner as technology changes.

Public purpose

Ownership should be understandable and repeatable.

The project is documented publicly to show how individuals and families can own more of their infrastructure, software, data, backups, and recovery paths.

PrivacyProtection is a design requirement.
OwnershipThe rightful owner retains authority.
Open SourceOpen foundations remain valuable and auditable.
RecoverabilityRestore paths are part of the architecture.
IndependenceProduct identity moves toward first-party GoreeCloud software.
PreservationImportant information should outlive the technology storing it.
