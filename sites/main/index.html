How GoreeCloud works

One platform. Multiple layers of control.

GoreeCloud separates access, applications, storage, security, and recovery so no single product becomes the platform itself. Technologies can be replaced while ownership, portability, and preservation remain constant.

01

Private Access

Approved users and devices receive only the connectivity required for their role.

02

Applications

Self-hosted and GoreeCloud-built applications serve family, knowledge, and operational needs.

03

Storage & Data

Important information remains organized, portable, and under direct GoreeCloud control.

04

Security & Isolation

Least privilege and workload separation reduce unnecessary access and blast radius.

05

Backup & Recovery

Independent recovery layers protect information from failures, mistakes, and migrations.

GoreeCloud principle: Systems are replaceable. The information they protect is not.

What is GoreeCloud?

More than a homelab.

GoreeCloud is a privacy-first, self-hosted personal and family cloud platform built to preserve a family's digital life over the long term.

Success is measured by whether information remains accessible, understandable, recoverable, portable, and under the control of its rightful owner as technology changes.

Public purpose

Ownership should be understandable and repeatable.

GoreeCloud is not a commercial cloud product. The build is documented publicly to show that individuals and families can own more of their infrastructure, software, data, backups, and recovery paths.

PrivacyProtection is a design requirement, not an optional setting.
OwnershipThe rightful owner retains authority over their information.
Open SourceOpen-source, self-hosted software is the default direction.
RecoverabilityRestore paths are part of the architecture, not an afterthought.
IndependenceCore software and infrastructure move toward direct GoreeCloud control where it provides durable value.
PreservationImportant information should outlive the technology storing it.
