Privacy at GoreeCloud

Privacy starts with collecting less.

The GoreeCloud public website is intentionally static and does not require an account. No behavioral tracking. The reviewed source does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party browser resources such as remote scripts or external web fonts.

01

No sign-in here

This public information surface does not implement GoreeCloud Identity sessions or authenticated application access.

02

No browser tracking stack

The reviewed website source does not add analytics SDKs, advertising tags, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime JavaScript.

03

Same-origin runtime assets

Browser-loaded site resources are kept on the GoreeCloud origin. CSS, JavaScript, GLAZE UI assets, and branding used by the deployed site are published with the site rather than fetched by the browser from unrelated origins.

04

External links are explicit

When you choose external links and leave GoreeCloud for another website, that destination has its own privacy and operational policies.

Local preference

Appearance can stay on your device.

If you explicitly choose Light or Dark appearance, the site stores that preference in browser localStorage under the key goreecloud-appearance. System mode requires no stored preference; selecting System removes that key and follows the device or browser color-scheme preference.

This local appearance preference is not an account identifier and is not sent by the website JavaScript to GoreeCloud.

Browser and hosting boundary

Minimize what the page can disclose.

The public response policy includes Referrer-Policy: no-referrer, restrictive script and connection policies, and same-origin browser assets. These controls reduce browser-origin leakage but do not imply that the network path carries no metadata.

Cloudflare Pages hosts the public static site and may process ordinary HTTP delivery information as the hosting layer. GoreeCloud does not claim that public web hosting produces zero infrastructure metadata.

Authority boundary

Privacy-minded source is not Privacy Shield acceptance.

Privacy Shield is GoreeCloud’s platform privacy authority. This website follows privacy-preserving design choices, but those choices do not manufacture a Privacy Shield runtime acceptance state.

For general public-website privacy questions, use goreecloud@gmail.com. Security concerns should follow the separate security-reporting policy rather than being sent through ordinary privacy contact.
