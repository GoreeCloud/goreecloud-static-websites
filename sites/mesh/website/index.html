GoreeCloud Mesh is the first-party coordination fabric for service and capability discovery, relationship and dependency modeling, policy-aware coordination, lifecycle state, events, health-aware impact context, and minimized evidence transport across participating GoreeCloud systems.

Explicit coordination

A shared model of how GoreeCloud fits together.

Mesh replaces hidden coupling with registered capabilities and explicit relationships. Discovery does not grant authorization, and coordination does not become authority transfer.

01

Registry

Records applications, services, capabilities, dependencies, endpoints, health, lifecycle state, and platform-conformance context.

02

Graph

Models direct relationships and computes dependency-impact context so cross-system effects can be explained instead of guessed.

03

Policy

Fails closed when actors, targets, capabilities, availability, or enabled relationships do not satisfy the registered contract.

04

Events

Moves bounded relationship and lifecycle events while keeping application payload ownership and business rules with the source system.

05

Nodes + connections

Represents service identity, operational state, and explicit versionable relationships without manufacturing ambient trust.

06

Platform catalog

Joins authority, contract, and conformance context for integral platform systems while preserving each producer's domain authority.

Evidence plane

Move evidence without inventing truth.

Mesh can preserve and transport bounded producer-authoritative evidence, but valid transport never upgrades the underlying security, privacy, continuity, identity, authorization, or design-conformance claim.

Source attestations

Exact-source provenance remains independent from runtime evidence and cannot satisfy Stable gates by itself.

Runtime evidence

Observed contract evidence is bound to canonical repositories, contracts, exact revisions, and observation time.

Evidence envelopes

Immutable transport records preserve producer, authority, assertion, freshness, minimization, subject scope, and optional digest binding.

Authenticated delivery

Write scope is not enough to impersonate another producer; verified service identity must match the evidence producer.

Subject views

Consumers can inspect current authority-separated evidence without receiving a manufactured overall protection or conformance verdict.

Private-first API

Discovery, registration, graph inspection, platform evidence, producer evidence, and policy evaluation remain private-first interfaces.

Authority boundaries

Connect the systems. Do not collapse them.

Mesh coordinates across first-party systems while their specialized responsibilities stay where they belong.

GoreeCloud Manager

Central administration and operational management remain Manager responsibilities.

GoreeCloud Identity

Authentication, authorization, accounts, devices, credentials, sessions, and delegated authority remain Identity responsibilities.

Wardveil Security

Protection, trust, verification, detection, response, and security evidence remain security responsibilities.

Privacy Shield

Privacy authorization, minimization, purpose limitation, retention boundaries, and user control remain privacy responsibilities.

Everkeep

Backup, recovery, preservation, portability, succession, and continuity remain resilience responsibilities.

Glaze UI

Presentation requirements and design-conformance authority remain Glaze UI responsibilities.

Core invariantauthority_transfer = false

Relationship discovery and coordination are never an authentication bypass, transport shortcut, permission grant, or authority to mutate another system.

Current implementation boundary

Native foundation in source. Production acceptance stays explicit.

Still gated

  • Production Monitoring, Gateway, Network, TLS, and DNS integrations
  • Multi-node replication and target-environment evidence delivery
  • Runtime acceptance for Glaze UI, Wardveil, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep where applicable
  • Production deployment, recovery, rollback, exact-revision approval, and Stable product qualification
