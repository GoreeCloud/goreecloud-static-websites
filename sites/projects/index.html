Native ownership.

Every GoreeCloud application is required to become an original native-from-the-ground-up GoreeCloud implementation. Existing complete upstream applications and forks may remain temporarily for migration, continuity, compatibility, testing, or reference, but they are not permanent GoreeCloud application architectures. Glaze UI, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Mesh, and GoreeCloud Identity are substantive platform systems with distinct responsibilities, expressed through the Design, Privacy, Security, Continuity, Mesh, and Identity Center model. Stable claims remain tied to current implementation and evidence.

Development model

Native is the required end state.

Every GoreeCloud application must be original GoreeCloud-owned software at the product and application layer. Mature libraries, standards engines, codecs, rendering engines, cryptographic primitives, operating-system/runtime components, and comparable foundations may remain when replacing them would materially increase technical risk. Transitional upstream product code stays identified as transitional.

Release integrity

Labels never outrun evidence.

Public source, a successful build, active development, a release candidate, or a platform identity does not automatically establish production acceptance or protection. Stable applications must satisfy current Glaze UI 2.1, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, and GoreeCloud Identity requirements for their supported scope where applicable. GoreeCloud Mesh may coordinate evidence and capability relationships, but it cannot create or upgrade another system’s authoritative state.
