Application identity

Canonical artwork, localized for dependable public delivery.

The Suite website uses reviewed canonical application identity when approved. Platform-specific derivatives may change size, mask, padding, rasterization, or adaptive layers while preserving the underlying approved identity.

If a future registry entry has no approved artwork, it must use a clearly neutral treatment until the branding authority publishes a canonical asset. Third-party infrastructure marks never substitute for GoreeCloud product identity.
