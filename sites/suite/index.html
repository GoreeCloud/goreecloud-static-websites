Application identity

One recognizable icon across supported surfaces.

The Suite website uses the reviewed canonical application identity when one is approved. Web/PWA, Android, desktop/Linux, future iOS, launchers, installers, updates, recovery tools, documentation, and repository surfaces may use platform-specific derivatives, but those derivatives must preserve the same underlying product identity.

If a future product has no approved artwork, it must use a neutral treatment until the branding authority publishes a canonical asset. Third-party infrastructure marks are separate: they identify external technology and are not GoreeCloud application icons.
