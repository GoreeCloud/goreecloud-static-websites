Product identity

Canonical artwork where approved. Neutral identity where it is not.

The Suite website uses reviewed GoreeCloud product artwork when approved. Products without an approved canonical asset use an intentionally neutral marker rather than a fabricated official logo.

Platform-specific derivatives may change size, mask, padding, rasterization, or adaptive layers while preserving the underlying approved identity. Third-party infrastructure marks never substitute for GoreeCloud product identity.
